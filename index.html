Research

Dissertation: A Real2Sim2Real System for Bimanual Long-Horizon Dexterous Manipulation via Temporal-Context Transformer Reinforcement Learning (Kyung Hee University, submitted October 2026).

1 · Real2Sim

A digital twin built from the real robot

The robot has a UR3e and a UR3 arm, each with a 16-DoF Allegro Hand V4: 44 joints controlled by one policy. Its digital twin uses the robot description, object meshes scanned with a phone and cleaned into collision geometry, and drive gains calibrated against motion recorded on the hardware. The same system was built for two configurations: table-mounted and upper-humanoid.

Object reconstruction figure

2 · Demonstrations

Thirty teleoperated demonstrations become three hundred

Demonstrations are recorded inside the digital twin. The operator wears two Manus gloves and three VIVE trackers; a learned retargeting network (GeoRT) maps the fingertips to the Allegro joints, and the wrist poses drive the arms through inverse kinematics. The thirty demonstrations per task are split into subtask segments, recombined into new episodes and replayed in the twin. Only physically valid episodes are kept, giving 300 per task.

Teleoperation pipeline · Finger retargeting · Demonstration stitching

3 · Policy

TC-TRL: two arms, one memory

Each arm with its hand is an agent. A temporal-context encoder attends over a window of recent observations and actions, so the policy knows which step of a long task is under way; an arm-conditioned decoder chooses the left arm's action knowing the right arm's. The policy is pretrained by behavior cloning on the demonstrations, then fine-tuned with PPO in parallel copies of the twin with domain randomization (upper-humanoid configuration: 300 demonstrations, 500 environments, about ten hours per task on one RTX 5070 Ti).

Simulation On the five long-horizon benchmark tasks of the table-mounted configuration (Isaac Gym twin), 89.9% mean success, against 59.0% for the best transformer baseline and 26.6% for the best PPO baseline.

IEEE RA-L 2024 (co-first author), presented at IROS 2025 · Korean Patent 10-2943203 · Architecture

4a · Direct transfer

The same policy, unchanged, on the real robot

A ROS 2 bridge runs the trained policy at 10 Hz. An RGB-D camera in front of the robot and a 6D pose pipeline (detection, segmentation, FoundationPose) supply the object poses the policy saw in the twin, and the 44 joint targets are smoothed and clamped before they reach the drivers.

4b · Distilled transfer (TMAD)

Dropping the pose estimator: a student that acts from two RGB cameras

The trained policy stays in the twin as a frozen teacher. A student that sees only two RGB images and the robot's proprioception is distilled from it online, in the states the student itself reaches, with randomized rendering. On the robot the student runs at 30 Hz with no pose estimator, no object model and no extrinsic camera calibration.

Code

Public repositories on GitHub. Results are as reported in each repository's README.

dexterous-pick-and-place

FoundationPose perception and force-closure grasp synthesis (BODex on cuRobo) driving an Allegro Hand on a UR3, in Isaac Lab and on the real robot.

Real robot 94 of 100 executed trials succeeded across 10 objects. Simulation 180 of 200 episodes, both arms.

Python · Isaac Lab · cuRobo · FoundationPose

dual-arm-allegro-ros2

ROS 2 Jazzy drivers for the bimanual torso: UR3 and UR3e arms, two Allegro Hand V4 over CAN, three RealSense D415 cameras, URDF of the stand and table, and bridges that take plain joint-position topics.

44 joints in one /joint_states at 100 Hz; the configuration the lab's robot runs daily.

C++ · ROS 2 · ros2_control · Docker

allegro-hand-retargeting

Real-time Manus glove to Allegro Hand retargeting for both hands, with a learned geometric model (GeoRT, trained without paired data) and a rule-based baseline, streamed to the hands through a 60 Hz ROS 2 bridge.

About 0.7 ms per hand per forward pass on an RTX 5070 Ti.

C++ · Python · PyTorch · ROS 2

foundationpose-ros2-tracker

Zero-shot 6D object tracking in ROS 2 from a mesh and an object name: YOLO-World detects, SAM 2 segments, FoundationPose registers and tracks, and world-frame poses are published for manipulation.

Includes a port of FoundationPose's CUDA extensions to Blackwell GPUs (CUDA 12.8). MIT license.

Python · ROS 2 · CUDA · SAM 2

quest3-dexterous-teleop

Meta Quest 3 hand-tracking teleoperation of the dual-arm, two-hand robot in Isaac Lab, with demonstration recording and a converter to a fixed-length imitation-learning dataset.

Simulation Teleoperation loop at about 30 Hz; 44-D actions.

Python · Isaac Lab · CloudXR · WebXR

FoldingACT

Vision and touch imitation policy for bimanual paper folding, built for the IROS 2026 Robotic Origami Challenge: an ACT-style action-chunking transformer over four cameras (DINOv3) and ten fingertip tactile pads (Sparsh), predicting 65-D joint targets.

Built the data pipeline for the 398 GB LeRobot dataset, an Isaac Sim mirror, and the inference service the organizers ran.

Python · PyTorch · LeRobot · Isaac Sim
